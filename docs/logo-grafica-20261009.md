## Prompt final

Use case: logo-brand. Create a single polished original logo for the Portuguese internal print production management system called GRÁFICA. Asset: clean horizontal logo lockup suitable for a software login page and navigation header. Transparent background with actual alpha. Symbol: an elegant distinctive geometric letter G constructed from two overlapping sheets of paper and a precise fold, communicating print workflow and organized production. Place symbol beside the exact wordmark "GRÁFICA" (accent on Á), professional bold modern sans serif with refined spacing. Match the application's simple neutral near-black identity; single-color dark ink shapes, flat vector-like rendering, strong silhouette and legibility at small sizes. No extra words, no slogan, no NORTE text, no mockup, no background scene, no border, no shadows, no gradients, no 3D, no watermark. Balanced proportions with clear negative space, modest transparent margin. One final logo only.
